FAQ
 What is the daily schedule of the Summer Intensive?                                 
The Summer Intensive (SI) runs Monday-Friday. SI students are placed in either an AM or PM cohort. The AM cohort takes place Monday-Friday from 9am-1pm EST. The PM Cohort takes place Monday-Friday from 11am-4pm EST. Office hours are offered Monday-Friday from 4-5pm EST and are strongly encouraged, though optional.
 
 Where does the Summer Intensive take place?                                 
The Summer Intensive is a fully live-remote program. Instruction takes place in our virtual classroom via Zoom and is highly engaging and interactive.
 
 Why should I apply to the Summer Intensive?                                 
The Summer Intensive is your chance to take action toward your future. It is more than just learning to code—it is about preparing for the opportunities that lie ahead. Here is why you should apply:
  • Build Real Skills: Gain hands-on experience creating web applications with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, all in an interactive, live-remote setting.
  • Develop Your Career Toolkit: Master essential professional skills like networking, resume writing, and presenting with confidence.
  • Expand Your Network: Meet tech professionals, hear their stories, and get advice from leaders who want to see you succeed.
  • Access the Tools You Need: Eligible students can receive laptops and Wi-Fi hotspots to ensure nothing holds you back.
  • Join a Vibrant Community: Learn alongside a cohort of driven peers who will challenge and inspire you.
Seats are limited, and the clock is ticking! Apply today to claim your spot in this life-changing program, where you will gain the confidence and tools to launch your digital future.
 
 What is the application process?                                 
The application window will open on February 3rd and will close on May 2nd. Applicants will be notified of our decision on a rolling basis. The application helps us get to know you, allows you to tell some of your story & flex your problem solving and perseverance skills. If you encounter difficulties during any part of your application process, please contact us. We are here to help!
 
 Is there a GPA requirement?                                 
No! Our All Stars shine in all sorts of ways, and grades are just one way to shine. GPA is NOT used in our admissions process.

We do collect GPA data for our statistics, though!
 
 Where do students end up after the Summer Intensive?                                 
After completing the Summer Intensive, many students continue building their programming skills through freelance work, college internships, or working as a seasonal instructor here at All Star Code, among many other opportunities. Additionally, all Scholars (program alumni) receive invitations to join our Scholar Portal for exclusive internships, jobs, and workshops.

Note for Scholars: If you have already attended our Summer Intensive program, please do not reapply. Students are only allowed one Summer Intensive experience. Scholars (program alumni), please check out our Scholar Portal for exclusive opportunities!
 
 When will I know if I was accepted into the program?                                 
Applicants can expect a notification of acceptance between 3-6 weeks after applying through our admissions portal. We try our best to process applications quickly.

Seats are limited—apply early to secure your spot in this life-changing program!
 
 What programming languages are taught?                                 
All Star Code is primarily based around web development, using technologies such as HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
 
 I already know how to code. Am I eligible?                                 
Yes! If you have prior coding experience, you may find the program even more rewarding. Contact admissions@allstarcode.org with specific questions about your skills.
 
 Can I join the Summer Intensive if I'm not in high school yet?                                 
Our program is specifically designed for students in high school. If you're not yet eligible, or aren't sure about applying just yet, please check out our virtual coding challenge, Crack the Code!
 
 I don't go to a traditional school (home schooled, not attending, etc.), am I still eligible?                                  
Yes! Applicants who are not currently attending high school will still be considered if they are 15, 16 or 17 years old. When filling out the form, please indicate your situation under the question "What is the name of your current high school?"

Please send inquiries to admissions@allstarcode.org, if you have questions about your application.
 
 Will there be a waiting list?                                 
Absolutely! Given the limited number of seats available, many applicants will qualify for the program but will not be selected in the first round. Applicants on the waiting list will be invited to join as spots become available.
 
 How can I improve my chances? What does it take to be an All Star?                                 
All Stars:
  • Respect others
  • Demonstrate logical thinking
  • Demonstrate growth mindset
  • Look up terms they're not familiar with (like "Growth mindset" )
  • Embrace "Celebrate Failure" = are hungry to learn by stepping out of their comfort zone
  • Embrace "Dare Greatly" = are ready to take risks
  • Embrace "Tell Your Story" = can convey ideas clearly and see value in doing so
  • Uplift others around them

In addition to this, we encourage you to participate in our virtual coding challenge, Crack the Code! It serves as a great introduction to some of our Summer Intensive topics.
 
 What is your attendance policy?                                 
The Summer Intensive is a rigorous and fast-paced program that requires full commitment. Your success and the success of your peers depend on your consistent engagement and participation. To ensure every student can make the most of this opportunity, we maintain the following attendance policy:

  1. First Week Attendance

    • Attendance is mandatory for the first week.
    • Missing the first day of the program will result in forfeiting your seat.
    • Arriving more than 30 minutes late on Day 1 will also result in forfeiting your seat.

  2. Absences and Lateness

    • Students are allowed up to three absences during the program.
    • Students must attend Office Hours to make up for any missed material.
    • Three instances of lateness (arriving after the scheduled start time) will count as one absence.

  3. Communication and Accountability

    • If you anticipate being absent, communicate with the All Star Code team in advance to avoid penalties.
    • Parents/Guardians will be notified after each absence to ensure accountability.

  4. Zero-Tolerance Policy for Week 1

    • Due to the importance of the first week, any unexcused absence or lateness during this period will result in dismissal from the program.
The Summer Intensive is a unique and rewarding experience, but it requires dedication. We are here to support you every step of the way—so bring your focus, commitment, and enthusiasm to make the most of this opportunity!
 
 What is your expulsion policy?                                 
All Star Code is committed to fostering a supportive, inclusive, and professional environment for all participants. To maintain the integrity of our program and ensure a positive learning experience, the following guidelines are strictly enforced. Students may be expelled from the program for any of the following reasons:

  • Attendance Violations:

    • Missing any day during the first week of the program without prior clearance from the ASC team.
    • Accruing three unexcused absences. For each absence, a student’s Parent/Guardian will be informed, and the student must attend Office Hours to catch up on missed material.
    • Three instances of lateness (defined as not being present by the scheduled start time) equal one absence. A student who logs in more than 30 minutes late to their virtual classroom is considered absent.

  • Project & Communication Requirements

    • Failing to submit weekly project assignments and being unresponsive to outreach efforts via phone or email.

  • Behavioral Violations:

    • Using abusive, intimidating, or discriminatory language toward instructors, peers, or program guests.
    • Exhibiting disrespectful behavior, including interrupting or undermining others, being disruptive in class, or disregarding program guidelines.
    • Engaging in harassment, intimidation, or discrimination based on age, sexual orientation, gender identity, ethnicity, race, religion, or disability.
 
 Commitment to Respect and Safety                                 
All Star Code is dedicated to maintaining a respectful, harassment-free learning and work environment. Any behavior that compromises the safety or dignity of others—including verbal abuse, intimidation, or willful sabotage—will not be tolerated.

Note: Students who are expelled from the Summer Intensive program are ineligible for participation in future ASC programs.

For questions or concerns regarding this policy, please contact the ASC team at admissions@allstarcode.org.
